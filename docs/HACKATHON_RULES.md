## Judging Criteria

From Val (xStocks team), what they most want to see:
1. **xStocks as collateral** — money market integrations
2. **Products previously gated in TradFi** — bringing institutional instruments on-chain
3. Clear articulation of why the idea matters and how it could scale
4. Composability with existing DeFi building blocks
5. New primitives enabled by tokenized equities

> "A strong submission doesn't need to be live — it needs to be convincing."
> — Leo, xStocks
